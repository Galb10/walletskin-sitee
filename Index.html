01 · CONNECT

Connect your iPhone

Use a data-capable USB cable, unlock your iPhone, and tap “Trust This Computer” if prompted. AirCard requires Windows 10/11 64-bit and Apple device support.

02 · SCAN

Identify your card

Open AirCard’s Wallet tab and click Scan. Open Wallet on your iPhone, tap the card you want to customize, then click Stop in AirCard.

03 · APPLY

Apply your Skin

Choose your PNG, JPG, or WebP artwork, adjust the crop, and click Apply Card Skin. Force-close Wallet and reopen it to check the result.

Compatibility and results can change with iOS updates. Back up your iPhone first. This is an independent guide and is not affiliated with Apple.

Compatibility

Before you buy.

AirCard currently documents support for Windows 10/11 64-bit and iOS 18 through 27.0.1, with additional beta support noted by the project. Apple patched the underlying exploit in iOS 27.2 beta 3+, so compatibility can change with future iOS releases.
